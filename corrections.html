Corrections

A misquoted verse, a wrong date, or a claim that does not hold up should be corrected.

On this pageWhat counts as a correctionOnline reportingHow corrections are handled

What counts as a correction

Anything factually or scripturally wrong. Most usefully:

  • A verse quoted incorrectly, or attributed to the wrong reference
  • A date, name, place or number that is wrong
  • A claim in “Today’s World” whose source does not support it
  • A link that goes somewhere unexpected
  • Something on the page that is unreadable or unusable for you

Disagreement about interpretation is welcome too, but it is a conversation rather than a correction. It will not change the page.

Online reporting

i Online correction reports are not available yet.

When reporting becomes available, the most useful details will be the study date or title, the section, what appears to be wrong, and any source that supports the correction.

How corrections are handled

  • Factual and scriptural errors are corrected on the page itself, not annotated at the bottom.
  • Substantive changes are noted so a reader who saw the earlier version is not confused.
  • Typographic fixes are made silently.
  • Interpretation is not corrected, though it may be reconsidered.
