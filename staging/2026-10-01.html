God's Spokespeople

The Creator SpeaksLesson 1Day 6 of 7

In brief

East of Eden, with a sword of flame at their backs, the human family might have expected silence. What they got was a God who would not stop talking. Today follows His voice through four channels. The sky overhead. Prophets with dreams and visions. A Book He breathed out. And at last a Man from Nazareth who could say that anyone who has seen Him has seen the Father. God did more than send messages. In the end, the Message came Himself.

Yesterday

Yesterday the gate closed. God spoke a promise, clothed the guilty, and sent them east with cherubim guarding the way back. At Sinai His voice came as thunder behind a fence. So how does a holy God keep talking to people who cannot come near?

Historical Context

Prophets Borrowed the Words of Royal Messengers

Long before phones, a king's word traveled with a messenger. Sometimes he carried a sealed letter, and sometimes only his memory. Either way he delivered it in the king's own voice, speaking as I and not as he. He opened with a fixed formula. Thus says the king. You can hear it in Scripture when an Assyrian officer stands outside Jerusalem's wall and announces what the great king says (2 Kings 18:19). Jacob's servants use the same form on a smaller scale (Genesis 32:4).

Israel's prophets took over that formula. Thus says the LORD appears hundreds of times in the Old Testament, and every time it sounded, the hearers knew what kind of speech they were getting. The prophet was not sharing his religious opinions. He was a messenger from a throne room, and the words belonged to the King. That is also why a false prophet was so dangerous. He was a courier delivering letters the King never wrote (Jeremiah 23:21).

Into the Word

Think about how you come to know someone you cannot visit. You might start with things they have made. Then come letters, and people who know them, and at last the day they walk through your door. Each step tells you more than the one before. Scripture describes God's speech outside the garden in a similar order (Hebrews 1:1-2). Begin with the widest channel, the one that reaches everybody.

Dreams fade and prophets die. So God made sure the messages would outlive the messengers.

Still, a letter is not a face. One night a disciple said out loud what people had wanted since Eden.

Street Level

The Donor Stayed to Stack Chairs

Imagine a small-town high school with a scholarship nobody can explain. For thirty years someone has paid for it, and for a new library wing as well, and no plaque carries a name. The building says this much. Somebody out there has means and wishes these kids well. It cannot say who.

Each spring a lawyer drives in and reads a short statement at graduation. He reads it in her voice, not his own. I am proud of you. I expect you to use this well. The office keeps every statement in a binder, and each new senior class pages through it. So thirty classes have had her words, exactly as she sent them, long after the ceremony ended.

Then one June an old woman in the third row stands up, and the lawyer sits down. She has brought no statement. She shakes every hand. She knows which boy lost his father in March. When the gym empties, she is in the back stacking folding chairs with the janitor. Nothing in the binder turned out to be wrong, and the school still keeps it. But a binder cannot stack a chair. Her statements had told them she was generous. Ten minutes in the room showed them she was kind. The prophets carried true words from God. In Jesus, the One who sent the words walked into the room.

Today’s World

The Musicians Were in Only One of the Rooms

On January 17, 2024, 130 first-year arts students from Erasmus University went to the same two short concerts at De Doelen, a concert hall in Rotterdam. Musicians from the Rotterdam Philharmonic Orchestra played the classical set, and a pop set followed. Researchers had split the students at random. Sixty-six sat in the room with the musicians. Sixty-four sat in another room of the building and watched a live stream on a large screen, with comparable seats, lighting, and sound.

Antonia Becker and her colleagues published the results in 2026. The students in the room with the players rated the music higher and reported feeling more moved, more energized, and more positive. Their average heart rate ran a little higher too, though about a quarter of the heart data was lost and the finer heart measures showed no difference. This was a small pilot with young arts students, and it does not make a stream false or worthless. It shows something narrower. The same music at the same moment reached people differently when the players were in the room. Philip had Moses and the prophets, and every word was true. He still asked to see.

Christ at the center

Put the week in order. God spoke with Adam in the garden, and Adam hid. He spoke through creation, and people worshiped the scenery. He spoke through prophets, and many of them were ignored or killed. So God did what no one expected. The Word became flesh and made His dwelling among us (John 1:14). No one has ever seen God, John says, but the one and only Son has made Him known (John 1:18). Jesus is not the best of God's spokesmen. He is the One all the spokesmen were talking about, the Seeker from the garden with a human face. And at the cross the Son of God said what we least expected and most needed, in a language any sinner can read. He would rather die than lose us.

Application

Pick one of the four channels and give it two honest minutes today. Step outside and look up. Or read a few verses from one Gospel scene, asking one question. If this is what Jesus is like, what is the Father like? Put what you notice into one sentence. If you want more later, give it ten minutes.

Discussion Question

Creation, prophets, Scripture, and the Son each tell us something about God. Which has shaped your picture of Him most, and which least (think about where your idea of God actually came from)? What might change if Jesus became the lens for the rest?

Closing Prayer

Father, thank You that You would not stay silent. You wrote Your power across the sky. You sent men and women with Your words in their mouths. You breathed out a Book and kept it for us. And when we still could not see You, You sent Your Son. Forgive us for the days we have not listened to any of it. Tune our ears to the voice of the Shepherd, and teach us to test every other voice by His. Guide us into all truth by Your Spirit, and hold us in the hands no one can pry open. In Jesus' name, Amen.
