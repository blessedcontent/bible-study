Privacy & reader controls

In plain language: what leaves your device, what stays on it, and how to change either.

On this pageThe short versionWhat is collectedReader controlsNotificationsMistakes

The short version

There is no account, no login, no email list, and no way for anyone here to know what you have read. Three things are worth knowing in more detail: a cookieless page counter, an optional daily notification, and local display and reminder preferences kept in your browser.

  • Nothing you read is tied to you. No account exists, so no reading history exists.
  • Notifications are opt-in and can be turned off at any time.
  • Your theme and red-letter settings never leave your device.

What is collected

Every data flow on this site
What Why Where it goes
Page views To know roughly how many people read, and which studies GoatCounter. No cookies, no cross-site tracking, no advertising identifiers, no profile
Notification registration Only if you opt in. It is the address your device uses to receive notifications. Firebase Cloud Messaging; the token is stored in Firestore with timestamps, a truncated browser description, your selected reminder time, and your browser’s time zone
Notification choice So you are not asked twice Three small cookies on this site: the yes-or-no choice, whether registration succeeded, and whether setup encountered an error
Theme & red letter So the site looks the same next visit Your browser’s local storage. Never sent anywhere
Archive study list To list published studies Requested from this site. If that list is unavailable the page falls back to GitHub, which then sees the request
Fonts Typefaces used across the site Currently the Google Fonts service, which therefore sees the request
i Notification records do not have a fixed expiration date. Turning notifications off here attempts to remove the device token from Firestore and Firebase. The notification sender also removes registrations that Firebase reports as invalid. GoatCounter and Google Fonts handle requests under their own privacy policies.

Reader controls

Theme, red letter, and the daily reminder are also in the menu at the top of any page. The reminder time is under Notifications, just below.

Theme

Dark for low light, Paper for long reading, Light for bright rooms. Remembered on this device.

Words of Jesus in red

When off, His words stay marked by their quotation marks. Printed pages always use black.

Daily reminder

A daily reminder near the time you choose, if you turn it on.

Manage
Clear local preferences

Removes theme, red-letter, and notification-choice preferences from this browser. It does not stop an active notification registration. To do that, use “Turn notifications off” below.

Notifications

• Reminders are off for this browser.

Optional

Get a daily reminder for today’s study

The automated sender checks throughout the day and sends near your selected time. The reminder includes the study title and a link to read it.

  • Turning it on asks your browser for permission, and you can refuse.
  • You can turn it off again here, or in your browser, at any time.

You can choose a time once reminders are on. The default is 7:30 a.m.

  1. Open the site permissions beside the address bar.
  2. Allow Notifications.
  3. Reload this page.

Permission belongs to your browser, not to this site.

Mistakes

If something here is wrong, such as a misquoted verse, a wrong date, or a factual claim that does not hold up, it should be fixed rather than defended. Online reports are not yet available. The Corrections page explains the policy.
